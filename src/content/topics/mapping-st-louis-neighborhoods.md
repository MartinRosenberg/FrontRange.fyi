## Neighborhoods this site combines

- **Dogtown** - Cheltenham, Clayton-Tamm, Franz Park, and Hi-Pointe. Dogtown is the name on the parade, the bars, and the neighborhood association; none of the four official names shows up on a storefront.
- **Midtown** 0 Midtown + Covenant Blu-Grand Center. Grand Center (the Fox, Powell Hall, the Sheldon, the museums) reads as part of Midtown.
- **Downtown** 0 Downtown + Downtown West. The line at Tucker splits one continuous downtown.
- **Carondelet** - Carondelet + Patch. South Broadway runs through both.
- **Greater Dutchtown** - Dutchtown, Mount Pleasant, Marine Villa, and Gravois Park. The name Dutchtown South Community Corporation and others use for the whole area.

## Neighborhoods this site renames

- **The Grove** is officially Forest Park Southeast. Everyone, including the neon sign over Manchester, calls it The Grove.
- **The Delmar Loop** is officially Skinker DeBaliviere, and most of the Loop itself is in University City, outside the city limits.

## Districts that cross official boundaries

- **Cherokee Street** runs through Benton Park West, Gravois Park, Benton Park, and Marine Villa (now Greater Dutchtown on this site).
- **South Grand** straddles Tower Grove South and Tower Grove East.
- **Cortex** spans parts of the Central West End, Midtown, and Forest Park Southeast.
- **The Loop** is Skinker DeBaliviere plus the edge of the West End, with most of it in University City, outside city limits.

## How the maps here handle it

- Combined neighborhoods are drawn as one shape on the [neighborhood map](/neighborhoods/), with a note on each page saying which official neighborhoods it covers.
- Places on a boundary street get listed on both pages, so a Cherokee Street antique store shows up under Benton Park and Greater Dutchtown.
